## Project Overview

In this prototype, I will give the site a new layout to help our user have a better flow when browsing the page. Adapting the viewport so our user will have a easy time scrolling through and reading the information.

### Requirement 1

The website did not have a safety feature in regards to weather/trail conditions.

Implemented a tab where Maya hits the safety link from either header or footer on her mobile phone and takes her to a page that tells her what the conditions are (although simple), gives her a good idea on what to expect.

This helps Maya by giving her a good idea on what she should expect from the trails conditions.

### Requirement 2

There was no address in regards to the location of the place or a phone number.

Added the information of the location of the trail and a phone number.

This helps Maya by giving her information to get to the location at ease, and phone number helps her just incase she has any additional questions.

### Requirement 3

No call to action in terms of finding the trails easy and to plan it.

Adding a call to action that is there right when the site is loaded up.

This helps Maya by giving her a big button that takes her to the page that has the trail information. On the desktop prototype, it links to a page about the trails.

## Prototype Flow

Describe the user task your prototype demonstrates.

Starting point takes the user to the hero/landing page that gives them brief information and a "Plan your hike" button.

User reads the information and can either click the button or scroll through the page for a brief overview.

System/Interface is available and accesible through the desktop or mobile versions.

User will have more information from the index page about the trail that she wants to do and how to prepare for it.

---

### Decision 1

Layout looked spaced out and didnt have too much information.

Give the prototype more space to work with and emphesized on important information with headings and underline.

This gives the user a better idea on what is important and a better flow of the page.

### Decision 2

No picures for our user to look at, page looks bland and boring.

Add photos to make the page more neater to look at.

Images stimulate the user with nice photos, and also give them an idea of what the location looks like.

### Decision 3

Giving detailed information on the trails.

Adding brief informaiton about the trial and difficulty. Additionally, adding a button to a different page to give more details about that particular trail.

This helps the user by telling them if the trail is meant for them or not and giving them information.

### Accessibility Decision 1

Adding information in regards to the trail information to help clear up any confusion that the end user may have.

### Accessibility Decision 2

Making sure that the viewport of the users device is easy to read and works as intended in regards of layout and text/design.
