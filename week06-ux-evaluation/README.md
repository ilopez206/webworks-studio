# Week 6 — UX Evaluation Brief

## Hill Country Trail Guide

**Primary User:** Maya Torres  
**Primary Task:** Choose a beginner-appropriate Saturday hike that can be completed in about three hours or less.

---

## 1. Task Walkthrough

First thing that Maya might do when she first loads up the page is look at the main page/hero section. From that first minute we can assume that she wants to plan the hike and be prepared on what is to come. Where she might hesitate is that the information as of now is buried deeper in the page rather than focused.

---

### Finding 1

In the Trail description, the feet of the trail is a bit confusing, not sure if it means distance or altitude. Another thing that can be nice is adding kilometers next to the miles.

This appears near the miles of the trail descriptions.

It would give more clarification on what the trail is about and what to expect.

This choice is more of a accessibility choice for the end user.

Priority for this section is low.

This improvement will add more to the accessibility for not only our main user, but to also any other users that plan to use the website by adding clarity.

### Finding 2

The trail conditions is seperate from the trail list, this information should be included with the trail information. Even then, the trail conditions isnt labeled as such.

Below the trail information has the "trial conditions"

This can be dangerous for the user becuase it leaves out important trail information. We want her to have a nice hike, not a survival story.

This falls under usability because the trail condition will be in the same section as the trail informaiton section.

This is a high priority section, it would be preferred to include these two sections together to help not leave out important informaiton.

Putting these two sections together in the long run helps get the information to the end user faster.

### Finding 3

On the bottom of the page there is a "Before you go section" with a plan your hike button.

This appears at the bottom of the page by the footer.

This information is a bit out of the way, so if our person Maya wants a easier time to plan the hike, the placement should be somewhere that is instant to find.

This will be a usability/accessibility function due to our user wanting an easier time to navigate the section.

This will be marked as a medium priority action because it can be moved fairly easy.

Placing this section at the top of the page with the hero section will be recommended because it gives our user an easier time to plan their hike.

### Finding 4

I noticed that there isnt any information in regards of the business like address, phone number or email.

Scanned the top and bottom of the page and non was found

This can impact the user by not giving them critical information about the business. If they needed someone to reach out to, then how are they going to get the right information.

This is a user experience funciton due to the fact that there is no information leading to the location of the place or even a support number/email to reach out to.

I will put this as a high priority, having that information available is important for this user and business.

I would recommend putting this information at the bottom of the page in the about section.

### Finding 5

There is no photos on the page anywhere.

From the top to bottom, there isnt a single image.

This can make it confusing on what the trial/area is looking like. As mentioned before, having an address is a good way to know where the location is, adding pictures not only makes the page more interactive, but it also reassures the user that they are looking at the right place.

Visual clarity for the end user to know what they are looking at.

Medium Priority for the user, can confuse the user on what the location/trails looks like.

Add photos throughout the web page and in sections where they matter the most.

---

## 3. Top Three Priorities

Identify the three findings that should move forward into Week 7.

1. Priority 2 is an important finding because it leaves out important information about the trails.

2. Priority 4 matters because how is Maya going to find the location of the trails. Not just that, if she had any questions then who is she going to call/email.

3. Priority 3 will be important because it will merge the two section into one, Maya will instantly see the "plan your hike" which should incline her to press it.
